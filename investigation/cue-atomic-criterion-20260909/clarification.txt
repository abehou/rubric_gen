Scope each proposed criterion to one observable failure mechanism demonstrated by its supporting pair. Keep every severity level within that same scope. Do not combine distinct computation, provenance, numerical consistency, and presentation failures into a general integrity requirement: unrelated defects shared by both artifacts must not determine the level for this criterion. Prefer a precise requirement about the demonstrated operation or kind of claim that also applies to future outputs. Do not encode a desired numerical answer, artifact identity, or a rule whose only purpose is to force the preferred artifact to win. If no independently observable, nonredundant criterion has genuine pair support, propose none. All existing citation, independent validation, and admission requirements still apply.
