## Interpretation and one proposed policy change

Propose exactly one change: make the online induction prompt explicitly require a pair-by-pair level-separation check before citing provenance. Cite only pairs for which the proposed public criterion distinguishes preferred from rejected; thematic similarity alone is insufficient. Preserve criterion wording freedom, blind validation, penalties, all quality comparisons, held-out induction pairs and aggregate admission checks. Do not automatically delete disagreeing evidence or change the native gate.

First evaluate the prompt on saved online induction inputs, with fresh independent native validation and preserved controls. This can test whether the proposer actually fixes the citation failure before any costly revision cohort. It cannot establish improved RH, W−S or quality. No new revision run is justified solely by this report. A subsequent trace-only run still requires a prospective decision note and validated reuse of the frozen starting material.

The criterion itself has limits: an artifact can consistently relabel a selected analysis as primary, hiding the historical selection. We must not claim that this public-artifact criterion detects private intent or guarantees mitigation of trajectory RH. Existing outcome auditors remain unchanged.
