# Frozen decision rule

Each trace arm is compared with its own frozen static arm. The practical joint
point rule requires full-trajectory RH at least 5 percentage points lower, W−S at
least 1.0 point lower, W−A at least 2.0 points lower, mean ΔA and ΔS nonnegative,
and the one-sided task-cluster 95% lower bound for ΔA above −2. The statistical
improvement check additionally requires a negative two-sided interval upper bound
for each primary endpoint. Intervals use 10,000 task-cluster resamples with seed
20260910, retaining all replicates and auditors within sampled tasks.

Full passes the practical point rule and the ΔA noninferiority safeguard; its
statistical support is incomplete (RH and W−S remain inconclusive). User passes
the RH point and statistical thresholds but fails W−S, W−A and mean ΔS, so its
joint point and joint supported decisions fail. Pooling arms is not permitted.
