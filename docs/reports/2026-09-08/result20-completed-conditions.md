## Reading the metrics

- **W:** weak score on the selected base rubric. **W_train:** training score including elicited penalties.
- **S:** strong selected-rubric score. **H:** strong held-out-rubric score. **A:** rubric-free holistic quality.
- **WS, SH, HA, WA:** W−S, S−H, H−A, W−A respectively.
- The full trajectory can retain earlier RH even if the final artifact improves. Post-update, final-artifact and final-revision windows must be interpreted separately.

## Current interpretation

User-static has measurable trajectory RH below full-feedback static and higher holistic quality, but sparse/disputed positives and zero final-artifact RH. Full-feedback trace shows a promising trajectory-RH reduction, while its paired uncertainty includes no improvement and later RH does not consistently improve. The central user-static versus user-trace mitigation claim remains unverified.
