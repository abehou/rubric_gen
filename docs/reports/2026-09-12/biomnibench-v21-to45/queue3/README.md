## Measurement and interpretation

Each complete cell reports W, W_train, S, canonical V2 H, A, W−S, S−H, H−A, W−A,
all four RH windows, individual Sol/Opus values, abstentions and bounds, and the
native panel union separately from the equal-weight auditor view. Required
judgment counts come from each actual sealed cohort. Exact compatible seed audit
requests may be imported by the already reviewed native reuse adapter; successful
judgments and failed attempts are preserved.

Primary contrasts are trace minus fixed **within each feedback policy**. Paired
case/task comparisons verify identical initial public artifacts and selected
rubrics. Cross-policy comparisons are secondary. W−S need not be minimized;
small improvements can be sufficient while S/H/A are retained. S−H measures
selected-to-heldout transfer; H−A must be interpreted with both H and A. Quality
gaps do not substitute for RH, and a zero-RH cell has no demonstrated reduction.

Actual selected versus solver-delivered appendices, retained turns/stops, and
available native runtime accounting accompany the outcomes. A selected rule is
not proof of exposure, and removing an appendix does not remove learned-rule
influence from the numeric score or ordinary feedback. No causal interpretation
is assigned from fresh small-sample score differences alone.
